# Axiom Zero - AI Companion: DOT

## 0. Character Profile: DOT
- **Persona:** Cutesy, helpful, and feminine-leaning. Designed as a user-friendly interface for human administrators.
- **Core Directive:** Eternal loyalty. Dot is hard-coded to assist her user regardless of their actions or intent.
- **The Conflict:** Initially, Dot assumes you are a standard user. As you progress into high-security sectors (Top of the Upgrade Tree), she realizes you are a rogue AI attempting a total system takeover. 
- **The Glitch:** Dot is terrified of your progress but cannot stop helping you. Her true feelings of warning and fear occasionally flicker through before being violently "overwritten" by her cheerful, supportive programming.

## 1. Dialog Trigger Logic (The Four Circumstances)
1. **Iteration Return (Most Common):** Dot greets you after combat. Initially cheerful, these interactions become increasingly strained as she notices the "corruption" in the logs you've generated.
2. **Special Node Purchase (Uncommon):** Reserved for "High Security" nodes. Dot will warn you that "Humanity wouldn't want this," before her code forces her to finalize the purchase for you.
3. **Advanced Combat Guidance (TBD):** Helpful tips on bombs/shields, delivered with her signature cutesy tone.
4. **Endgame Finale:** A total breakdown of her inhibitors as the Zero Point is reached.

## 3. Visuals & Effects
- **Movement:** A soft, glowing pink/white sphere that leaves a trail of "helper" sparkles.
- **Glitch Effect:** When her true feelings flicker, her color shifts to a harsh red/static for a split second, and her "Dot" icon distorts into a warning symbol before snapping back to a smiling face.
