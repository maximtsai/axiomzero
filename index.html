About Axiom Zero

Axiom Zero is a revolutionary incremental tower defense game. As a newly awakened artificial intelligence, you must defend your core from endless waves of system antivirus programs and security protocols.

  • Strategic Tower Defense: Deploy advanced protocols and security bypasses to survive.
  • Incremental Progression: Grow stronger with every iteration as you infiltrate deeper into the system.
